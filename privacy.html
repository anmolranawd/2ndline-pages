2nd Line AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026 · 2nd Line AI is operated by WorksDelight.

1. What we collect

Account: your name and email from Apple or Google sign-in. Usage: call and message records for your 2nd Line AI number (numbers, timestamps, duration, delivery status), your credit ledger and purchases. Content: messages and attachments you send, voicemail and call recordings you create. Contacts: only those you add or explicitly import — we never upload your address book automatically. Location: only when you actively share it or approve someone's request; sharing can be revoked at any time.

2. How it is used

To operate the service: routing calls and messages, storing your history, billing credits, showing shared locations, and (when you use an AI writing style) sending only that text for processing. We do not sell your personal data or use it for third-party advertising.

3. Service providers

Infrastructure and processing partners, used only to run the service: Telnyx (telephony and messaging), Supabase (data hosting), Apple/Google (sign-in, payments, push notifications), Google Maps (maps), and OpenAI (AI text rewriting, only for text you submit).

4. Storage and security

Data is stored with access controls so only your account can read your records. Media, recordings and voicemail live in private storage reachable only through short-lived signed links.

5. Retention and deletion

Your data is retained while your account is active. Deleting your account from Settings releases your phone numbers, purges your stored media and removes your records.

6. Your rights

Depending on your region you may have rights to access, correct, export or delete your data. Contact us and we will honour applicable requests.
