Before you write

Including these makes it much faster to help: the phone number in the app that the problem affects, roughly when it happened, and what you saw.

My second number is not ringing

Check Settings → Business Hours. When business hours are on and the call arrives outside them, it goes to voicemail by design. Also make sure notifications are allowed for 2nd Line AI in iOS Settings — calls arrive as a push, so a blocked app cannot ring.

How do credits work?

Credits are prepaid and are spent on calls, messages and buying a number. The current rate for any destination is shown on the Tariff screen before you call or send, and your balance is on the home screen. Buy more from Home → Credits.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple, so they are cancelled in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions — not in 2nd Line AI. Cancelling stops future renewals; the current period runs to its end.

Can I call emergency services?

No. 2nd Line AI cannot reach 911 or any other emergency number. Always use your phone's built-in dialer for emergencies.

What happens to my number if it expires?

A number is rented for a fixed period and shows its remaining days on the home screen. Renew it from there before it lapses, or turn on auto-renew. Once a number is released it may be taken by someone else and cannot be recovered.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Account → Delete Account. This removes your profile, messages, call history, recordings and voicemail. It cannot be undone, and unused credits are not refundable.

Reporting abuse
